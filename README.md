Solmar Bay is an original open-world crime game that runs entirely in the browser. You play Rico Vega, a fixer working the streets of a sun-soaked coastal city: explore it on foot or behind the wheel, weave through live traffic and crowds of pedestrians, steal parked cars, and watch the wanted level climb as police cars give chase. Three story missions, given by fixer Mara Quinn, take you from a harbor delivery to a high-speed pursuit of rival Dante Cross and a final heist, while pistol combat, a HUD with cash, health, ammo and a live minimap, and a save system that remembers your progress keep every run moving. Everything is built from scratch: the city, cars and characters are procedural three.js geometry, and all sound is synthesized WebAudio, no borrowed assets anywhere.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome and press START THE STORY. Any static server works too: serve the `game/` folder and open `game/index.html`. Controls: WASD to move or drive, mouse drag to orbit the camera, E to enter or steal a car, F to punch, click to shoot, R to reload, Shift to run, P to pause.
